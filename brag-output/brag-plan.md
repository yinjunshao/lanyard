# Lanyard — brag plan

**What it is:** A badge you show at events. People scan it and connect with a shared selfie, so a week later you remember the face, the name and what they can help with.
**For:** Students at careers fairs, freshers' fairs, society socials. Free, no app to download.
**Sets it apart:** Two-way connection that keeps the *context* — shared selfie, a note, "Come to me for" tags — instead of a one-way lead or a bare contact.
**Visual hook:** A wall of faces from last night fading into blank silhouettes.
**Real UI shown:** The Badge (strap, photo, "What I do", "Come to me for" chips, QR, code LNYD), the scan reticle, the Connect sheet (shared selfie, note, Connect), the People list.
**Tone:** `default` leaning `polished` — warm, clean, paper-and-ink badge language, one Persimmon accent.
**Share caption:** "You met 20 people at the fair. Lanyard makes sure you still know who they are next week."

## Angle
Lead with the user's problem — you meet lots of people at an event and forget them — then walk one real connection through the product: Maya shows her badge, Leo scans it, they take a shared selfie, and a week later Leo finds her by searching "CAD".

## Music
120 BPM (beat 0.5s, bar 2s), A minor → C major lift at the reveal. Every cut on a bar line or half-bar.
