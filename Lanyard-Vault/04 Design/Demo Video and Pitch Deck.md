# Demo Video and Pitch Deck

## Video ("brag") v3, 27.5 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 120 BPM
Hook (20 faces fading to silhouettes) → problem (contacts with no faces) → reveal (`lanyard.` "Remember everyone you meet.") → show + scan → connect with selfie → links in one tap → privacy beat ("People only see what you share.") → one week later, search "CAD" finds Maya → outro "Free for attendees, no app to download".
Share caption: "You met 20 people at the fair. Lanyard makes sure you still know who they are next week."
Files in `brag-output/` (v1 24.5 s, v2 25 s, v3 27.5 s); a dated copy in `brag-output-2026-10-27-012233/`.
