# Brand and Logo

**Mark:** a name badge with the lanyard slot at the top and an "L" cut out; slot and L are real holes so the mark takes the background.

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Lanyard Orange (Persimmon) | `#D9502B` |
| Text | Ink | `#1B1A17` |
| Background | Cream (Paper) | `#F4F1EA` |

- Wordmark: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Bold (SIL OFL), outlined, tightened spacing; written `lanyard.` with a full stop in the UI.
- Clear space: one slot-width. Minimums: symbol 16 px / 6 mm; horizontal 24 px high; stacked 48 px.
- Orange on cream/white is about 4:1: fine for the large mark, not for small body text.
- Do not recolour, add shadows/gradients, rotate, stretch, or place orange on red/orange or busy photos.
- Files: `logo-kit/` (SVG lockups, app icon, favicon set, manifest, `build.py` regenerates all).
