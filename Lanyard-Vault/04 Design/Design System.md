# Design System

**Concept:** a physical ID badge. Portrait card, punched slot, photo, labelled fields like a printed pass. Everything else stays quiet. See [[D010 Paper, ink and one strap colour]].

## Tokens
```css
--paper: #F4F1EA; --ink: #1B1A17; --graphite: #6B675F;
--rule: #DAD5CA; --strap: #C4441E;  /* Persimmon, deepened 2026-11-24 (F55.0): white on it is 5.0:1 */
--radius: 6px; --space: 8px;
--font: "Atkinson Hyperlegible Next", system-ui, sans-serif;
```

## Card colours (F55)
The strap's ten presets are Persimmon `#C4441E`, Graphite `#2B3138`, Navy `#1F3A5F`, Cobalt `#2F5BD3`, Teal `#12817A`, Forest `#2E7D4F`, Ochre `#9A6410`, Rose `#C23B66`, Plum `#7A3E8E` and Slate `#55606E`, each at 4.5:1 or better with white strap text. Swatches are round, 28 px dots in 44 px tap targets with a 2px ink ring (3px in from the edge) on the chosen one; the colour panel is a popover from 600 px and a sheet on a phone (UiSheet), with `--dur-fast` motion only. A card's colour reaches the page as data and is drawn with `--card-strap`, `--card-strap-ink` and a body `--ink`; strap words are white or black, whichever reads at 4.5:1.

## Strap materials (F56, Plus)
Six finishes for the strap, drawn with CSS only: Gold, Silver, Rose gold, Copper, Pearl, Carbon. Each is a 115 degree gradient of five tones with a soft highlight band at 50% (tones in `src/lib/cardmaterial.js`, the same in `main.css`), a light line along the top edge and a darker one along the bottom. Restrained: no texture except a faint twill on Carbon. Words on the strap are `#1B1A17` on the light metals and white on Carbon, at least 4.5:1 on every point of the finish with the highlight added (tested). The strap does not change with the app theme, so a material reads the same in Light, Dark and Midnight. The sheen is one layer moved with transform only: one 300 ms pass when the card appears, and on a desktop it follows the pointer (200 ms a move). It never loops, never asks for device orientation, and stays still under reduced motion.

## Emails (F58)
Every email is made by `emailLayout` in `worker/mail-layout.js` ([[D053 Every email goes through one layout, with no remote images, checked when it is sent]]). Paper `#F4F1EA` behind a 560 px raised card `#FFFDF8` with a 1px `#D8D2C5` rule and 6px radius; a 6px Persimmon `#C4441E` strap bar across its top; the wordmark "lanyard." in text (bold 20 px, the full stop in the strap colour); a 24 px bold title line; 16 px / 24 px ink `#1B1A17` body text, muted `#6B675F` for small print; the footer under a rule names who runs Lanyard (`OPERATOR`), why they got it and the stop link. One 44 px Persimmon button with white text, 6px radius, written as a table button with a VML twin for Outlook; a second action is the quiet kind (a 1px ink border, no fill). Facts (when and where, counts) sit in a bordered panel; the sign-in code is 36 px bold, spaced, in a panel. Dark (for clients that support `prefers-color-scheme`): paper `#141311`, card `#201E1A`, ink `#F4F1EA`, muted `#9D988D`, rule `#3A362F`, links and the wordmark's full stop `#EA7552`; a client that ignores it gets the light version, which is the inline one. Type is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next then the system stack, never fetched. No images (so no tracking pixel and nothing to block), no script, no stylesheet, no web font; every email under 30 KB. `npm run mail:preview` writes every email with sample data to `scratch/mail-preview/` (git-ignored) with an index.

### Event themes (F65)
Eight, from `src/lib/eventthemes.js`, drawn by `src/styles/eventthemes.css` on the check-in head, the Present page and the poster ([[D060 Event themes are one shared list drawn by CSS, and a chosen text colour must read]]). Persimmon `#C4441E`, Formal `#2B3138` and Night (dark card, Persimmon strap) are the three that always existed and look as they did. Forest `#2A6B45`, Ocean `#1F5F8B`, Plum `#6E3A82` and Rose `#B83A5E` carry white words (6.4, 6.8, 8.1 and 5.5:1) on a faint tint of the same colour (`#F1F6F1`, `#EEF4F8`, `#F6F0F8`, `#FBF0F3`); Sand `#A67C37` carries `#1B1A17` words (4.6:1) on `#FAF4E8`. A head is a card with its own paper, so it reads the same in Light, Dark and Midnight; Present is tinted in its light look only. A brand colour is any colour, and its words are white or black, whichever reads better (always 4.58:1 or more); a host's own White or Dark must reach 4.5:1 or is not offered. The theme grid is 44 px swatches in a radio group with a ring on the chosen one (opacity and transform, tokens).

## Rules
- One primary action per screen, bottom thumb zone, Persimmon.
- 1px borders not shadows; 4-6px radius; no hover effects; motion only as set out under Motion below (and the shutter flash on a selfie).
- Contrast 4.5:1 text; tap targets 44px; every input labelled.
- No gradients, glass, emoji, sparkle icons, "New" pills, or centred three-card hero.
- Tabular figures, no monospace; labels in small caps with slight spacing.
- Pickers: no native `<select>`, date or time inputs. Use `UiSelect`, `UiCombobox`, `UiDatePicker`, `UiTimePicker`, `UiDateTimePicker` from `src/components/ui/` (bottom sheet under 600 px, popover above). Same rules as the rest: 1px borders, no shadows, 44px targets, a bottom sheet that slides and a popover that fades, by the Motion tokens. See [[F08 Custom pickers and dropdowns]] and [[D018 Hand-built pickers outside the shared Vue chunk]].
- Motion (F40): sheets, panels, dialogs and tabs move, so a change reads as a change, and only in the ways below. See [[F40 Motion for sheets panels and tabs]] and [[D045 Motion is CSS tokens and a few lines of script, never a library or Vue Transition]].
- Rotating examples (F52): an empty text field may show an example that swaps for the next every 3.5 s: the old line rises and fades out (140 ms), the new one rises in from below (200 ms), by `--shift` and the tokens, so under reduced motion it is a fade. The overlay is `aria-hidden`, takes no clicks, goes at the first keystroke, and the input keeps a static placeholder.
- Tag boxes (F53): a tag the person types becomes a chip (the chip style, with a small remove button) inside one field-looking box; a new one eases in with the pop token and a removed one fades out, both on the motion tokens. Suggestions are five a mode under the field, and one already added is hidden.
- Opening in place (F57): a card editor in My cards, and the New card form, ease open: the row's height goes from nothing to its size in 200 ms (a grid row, 0fr to 1fr, the one height ease besides `<details>`), the content fades in and its parts rise in 30 ms apart; closing is 140 ms, and the editor leaves the page only after that. Under reduced motion the height does not move and the content fades. Focus goes back to that card's Edit button.
- Create event steps and perk rows (F63): a step slides in from the side you are going towards (24 px, three times `--shift`, with a fade over `--dur`), Back the other way, and under reduced motion it is the same fade with no distance; the step heading, which holds focus, never moves. A step is labelled groups (a small-caps heading, even 16 px gaps, one muted help line under each field, a 1px rule between groups), and the five step names are a progress row (a 3px bar on top of each, Persimmon for the current and the done). A tick that reveals more (Food or drinks provided, the attendance list, the feedback editor) eases its block open with `UiReveal`. Perks are rows of two boxes, a narrow Perk and a wide Details (optional) (1fr and 2fr from 480 px of the form's own width, a container query, and stacked name-first below it), with a text Remove button named for its perk and an Add a perk button; a new row rises in and a removed one fades out on the tokens.
- Check-in page rhythm (F67): the page is three panels, About you, At this event and Sharing, each drawn only when it has something, with one gap between panels (`--panel-gap`, 24 px) and one gap between rows inside a panel (`--row-gap`, 12 px), both set once on the page. Every tick is a full-width bordered row at least `--tap` (48 px) tall, with the small print under it closer to it than the next row; a group inside a panel has a quiet small-caps sub-heading (Who to meet, Sponsors, This event collects details). No inline styles: the only inline value is the host's own strap colour on the head.
- App themes (F54): Auto, Light, Dark and **Midnight**. Midnight is `#000000` paper for OLED screens: raised surfaces `#0d0d0d`, rule `#262422`, muted text `#9a958b`, the Dark ink and accent; things that float get a 1px rule edge instead of a shadow, bordered things none. Under Auto, a second choice picks Dark or Midnight when the device is dark. Every dark rule names both `night` and `midnight`; a card's own Night theme is separate and looks the same in every app theme.
- Elastic (F42): a sliding bar (the tab bar's bar, and a segmented control that glides, the host event tabs and Settings' appearance) overshoots slightly and settles, on `--ease-spring` (a `linear()` spring, about 7% overshoot, with a `cubic-bezier` fallback) over `--dur-elastic` 420 ms. That is the one named exception to the 300 ms rule; nothing else may be over 300 ms, and the elastic token is never over 450 ms. A tap while the bar is still moving, or a third tap within about 600 ms, jumps it with no transition, and the next calm tap springs again. Only real sliders get it; chips and plain segmented pickers still change at once. See [[D046 Elastic spring for sliding bars, with a spam skip]].
  - Only `transform` and `opacity` move (a `<details>` may also ease its height where the browser can). Nothing runs over 300 ms, and a thing leaves faster than it arrives. Durations and curves are the tokens in `:root` (`--dur-fast` 120 ms, `--dur` 200 ms, `--dur-sheet` 260 ms in, `--dur-sheet-out` 200 ms out, `--ease-out`, `--ease-in`); never write a time in a component.
  - A phone sheet slides up from the bottom and down again, with its dim fading beside it; a centred dialog or a popover drops 8 px and fades; the preview panel slides in from the right. Closing is quicker than opening. A dialog stays in the page until its exit ends and takes no clicks while it leaves; the page behind and focus wait for the exit.
  - A phone sheet can be swiped down by its handle or, with the content at the top, anywhere; past 30% of its height or a flick closes it, a short drag springs back, and scrolling its content never closes it.
  - New content fades in; the first six rows of a list rise in 30 ms apart; a new page in the signed-in frame fades in over 120 ms; one bar slides between the tabs; a `<details>` eases open.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` turns every slide into a short fade (one block in `main.css` redefines the tokens). No animation library and no Vue `<Transition>`: both cost public pages bytes.
- Waiting: grey blocks in the shape of the content (`--rule` on paper, no new colour) with one soft sweep that stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the region is `aria-busy` with one polite message; after about 8 seconds it says it is taking longer than usual and offers Try again; a button that sends is disabled and says "Saving…". Public pages do this with CSS only. See [[F38 Loading states]] and [[D043 Waiting states are CSS on public pages and one component elsewhere]].
- Errors: say what happened and what to do, in plain words, with no code, status number or library English, no blame and no exclamation mark; the Worker writes them, the vague ones stay vague. See [[F39 Informative error messages]] and [[D044 Errors say what happened and what to do, and the vague ones stay vague]].
- Performance: under 50 KB JS on public pages; profile pages under 1 s on 4G.
