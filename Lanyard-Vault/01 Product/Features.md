# Features

## Attendee side
- **Student and Career mode:** a profile is either a student (course and university) or working (company and role, with where they studied optional). Switching keeps everything typed; the card, people list and search show only the chosen mode (F11).
- **Welcome steps** (F14): a new card is made one question a screen at `/app/welcome`: name and photo (F51: no tick; a line above Continue says that pressing it confirms 18+ and agrees to the Terms and Privacy Policy), Student or Working, what you can help with and one line, links, save your card (email by code, then an optional passkey), and a done screen with the badge. Each answer is saved as it is given, so the steps resume on any device; every step after the first can be skipped, except save your card without an email (F45: a typed email is kept unconfirmed until the link in our first email is pressed; "Confirm now with a code" still works) or while a promo code is waiting. A guest who connected before making a card is asked to finish by name. [[D023 Welcome steps keep their place on the server and count only]].
- **Email when someone new connects** (F45): a guest connecting from a card gives a name and an email; only Lanyard sees it, it cannot sign in until confirmed by the link in our first email, it gets at most two emails until then, and it is cleared after 30 days if never confirmed. "Already on Lanyard? Sign in" on the sheet comes back to the card. [[D049 A new person's email is kept unconfirmed, never signs in, and gets at most two emails]].
- **Institution picker:** University, Studied at and a society's university are searched from a UK, US and world list (about 1,000 universities; names, short names such as UCL or MIT, accents ignored; the country shown after a non-UK name; written by an agent, owner to review) or typed if not listed; an email like name@students.example.ac.uk suggests its institution. The list was written by an agent and needs owner review (F12, [[D021 Institution list from knowledge, loaded when the picker opens]]).
- **Cards:** one profile, many cards ("Careers fair", "Hackathon", "Weekend"). Each card has its own note, visible links, tags, theme, QR and code. Free plan: 3 cards; Plus: 12. See [[D015 Profile split from cards]].
- **Card code in the card editor** (F33): each card's code, the Plus custom code and the random replace live in that card's editor in My cards; My card shows the code and a link to it.
- **Rotating codes:** 4-character card code rotates every 3 days (6h grace). See [[D016 Rotating card codes]].
- **Connect with shared selfie:** consent from both people, deletable by either. See [[D005 Shared selfie needs both consents]].
- **How you met** (F13): each connection says how it was made (QR code, code or link), at which event if you were both checked in, and when. Shown to the two people only; old connections just say Connected.
- **Private notes** per connection; optional "personal note" on a card.
- **People:** grouped by event and date, keyword search (name, tags), filter.
- **Messages/threads** on a connection (table `messages`).
- **Report and block** on any profile or connection.
- **Create event in steps** (F30): the host creates an event one stage at a time (basics, look, at the event, questions, review) with the step in the address and a draft that survives a refresh; the check-in page preview stays beside it.
- **Named attendance** (F31, [[D038 Named attendance is per event, shown on the check-in page and pre-ticked by the owner's choice]]): a host can switch on, per event, a list of who checked in, with the person's verified email and card as optional extras and a required "why" line. The check-in page shows "This event collects" above the Check in button, with every tick already in and each one untickable; the host sees only people who left the tick on and only what they left ticked, plus a CSV; people can change their mind from the event hub or Settings and come off the list at once; the list is deleted 12 months after the event. Events without it look and answer exactly as before.
- **Drops for codes, keys and vouchers** (F32, [[D039 Drops are sealed with a Worker key, given by one conditional write, and vouchers are marked with a staff PIN]]): a host gives each checked-in person something only they can see: the same text for everyone (a Wi-Fi password), one each from a pasted list (API keys), a random one from a list, or a food voucher that staff mark as used with a PIN. People open theirs under "For you" on the event page, press Show and Copy; the host sees counts, and names only at an event that keeps a list of who checked in. Items are stored sealed with a Worker key and cleared 30 days after the event ends.
- **Live previews** (F18): hosts see the check-in page beside the event form as they type, with the theme and a brand colour; people see their badge and the card page while editing a card or profile. The real page drawn from the same data; nothing is saved or sent ([[D029 A preview is the real component fed from the same data]]).
- **Present page and poster** (F19): from the live tab a host opens a full-screen page for a projector (event, big QR, code, a check-in count that updates by itself) in a new tab, or prints an A4 poster. Neither shows anything about a person; both use the main site's check-in address ([[D030 Present and the poster share one route, one address and one QR path]]).
- **Shared ground** (F16):- **Shared ground** (F16): a person's card page has one "In common" line ("Both into robotics · Same course"), worked out from what each of you shows the other, with no AI and nothing stored. See [[D026 Shared ground is worked out from what each person shows, and never stored]].
- **Who to meet for everyone** (F60, [[D054 Everyone checked in can be suggested unless they untick Suggest me, shown only what their card shows]]): at check-in, an optional "What are you hoping to get from this event?" with rotating examples and the "Suggest me to people here" tick, on by default; on the event hub while the event is on, "Who to meet" shows up to five people checked in there (first name and last initial, headline, three tags, their intention, a reason, a photo only with Be easy to find), ranked by AI from everyone's intentions and cards when AI is on ("Ranked with AI", at most once every 20 minutes) and without AI otherwise. Untick on the hub or in Settings; the host can switch it off per event; hosts see two counts.
- **Event presence and who to meet** (F17; since F60 Be easy to find only adds a photo to Who to meet): at an event that is on, an optional "Be easy to find" (face photo, a line, a wish), seen only by others at the same event who turned it on, with a suggested-people row ranked from the wish and shared ground (no AI). Off by default; deleted when turned off or 48 hours after the event ends; hosts see only a count.
- **Events tab and event hub** (F15): the fourth tab lists what is on now and the events you attended (Ongoing or Ended, the date you attended, your connection count); checking in lands on the hub with your figures and the people you met there.
- **Event room, album and Discovery** after check-in. See [[Host Portal and Billing]].
- **Feedback survey** after events, with reminder email.
- **Profile score and finish-your-profile emails** (F49): Edit profile shows "Profile N of 6" with what is missing as links to the fields; someone whose profile is under 6 gets at most two emails after an event, timed to their evening, stopped by the reminders switch.
- **Account:** passkeys, email sign-in, authenticator app (TOTP), recovery code, export my data, delete account. See [[Auth and Sessions]].
- **Plus plan** (waitlist table exists): more cards, any card colour and a text colour. See [[Pricing]].
- **Card colours** (F55, [[D052 Free accounts save only preset strap colours; Plus any colour and a text colour, checked in the Worker]]): in the card editor the strap colour is a row of ten round preset swatches for everyone (Persimmon, Graphite, Navy, Cobalt, Teal, Forest, Ochre, Rose, Plum, Slate; each reads with white text at 4.5:1) and the name of the chosen one under it. Custom, at the end, is the Plus colour panel (a square for saturation and brightness, a hue slider, a hex box that takes #RGB or #RRGGBB, the last five custom colours; a popover on wide screens, a sheet on a phone; fully usable by keyboard; Done or Enter keeps, Escape cancels); a free account sees it marked Plus and is taken to the Plus page. Plus also has a Text colour (Auto, or Custom in the same panel): it says "Hard to read" under 4.5:1 against the card and will not keep one under 3:1. The card, the badge on My card, the live preview, the card page and the card image all draw it; the Worker enforces the plan rule, so editing the form does not get round it. A lapsed Plus account keeps what it saved.

## Host side
- Its own site and account (`host.lanyd.fyi`): sign in with an email code, no passkeys; setup is details, Host Terms, then own name or organisation, with Back and Sign out on every step (F10).
- Create / duplicate / end events; event QR and rotating event code; live check-in count; custom check-in questions (max 3).
- Aggregate report (PDF-style view and CSV).
- Sponsors on an event; sponsor opt-in at check-in.
- Feedback survey builder and results.
- Team members (owner, staff, sponsor_viewer), invites (by host email or code), ownership transfer by host email, support tickets.
- Webhook (signed) and RSVP API key. See [[Integrations and Email]].
- Billing requests (invoice / PO). See [[Host Portal and Billing]].

## Admin
`/admin` (token-protected): approve societies and institutions, grant credits, see usage, and the welcome-steps funnel (counts per step for 30 days, nothing about who).

## AI foundation (F21, built, off until the owner turns it on)
No AI feature is visible yet. The shared plumbing is: one Worker-side client, limits per person, a budget, a kill switch and an allow-list on `/admin`, a Settings switch "Let people I've connected with find me using AI search" (on by default) and the Plan page's allowances. See [[F21 AI foundation]] and [[AI Safety and Cost Plan]].

## Follow-up drafts (F22, built on the AI foundation, in progress)
On a connection's page, in its own "Write to {first}" block under Follow up (F47): "Draft a message" opens a sheet. Pick where it will go (LinkedIn or WhatsApp only if their card shows that link; Email always), a tone, a goal, up to 3 things to talk about (from their side, the top one chosen for you) and up to 2 you can offer, whether to use your private note, and a line of your own (300 characters). Tone and Goal each end with "Your own…" (F44: a few typed words, tone up to 15 characters, goal up to 40, letters, numbers, spaces and `' - , . ! ?` only) and Talk about has "Add your own point" (up to 3, 40 each); typed words reach the model only as fenced descriptions ([[D048 Typed tone, goal and points reach the model only as fenced descriptions]]). The draft is plain text in a box you can edit, with Copy, Open in LinkedIn, Mail or WhatsApp, Try again (uses an allowance) and Mark as followed up. It says it is a draft to check before you send it; Lanyard never sends it. When AI is off, a limit is reached, you are not let in yet or the reply fails its checks, you get a template ("Here's a starting point") with the reason, never an error. Free: 3 a day, 15 a month; Plus: 15 a day, 150 a month. Counts of drafts shown, copied, opened and marked are on `/admin`. See [[F22 AI follow-up drafts]] and [[D032 Follow-up drafts are built from database rows and checked on the way out]].

## Do I know someone who (F24, built on the AI foundation, in progress)
On People, under the search box: "Do I know someone who...". Say in your own words who you need ("someone who works in climate policy", up to 200 characters), press Search, and get up to five people you are already connected with, each with a one-line reason and where it matched (their tags, their headline, your own note...). It looks only through your own connections, only at what each of them shows you, and never at anyone who switched AI search off in Settings or is blocked. Found people link to their card. If AI is off, used up for the day (5 free, 30 on Plus) or fails, the same sheet ranks the people already on the page by keyword and says so. "Not right? Search by keyword" puts the words in the People search box. See [[F24 Do I know someone who]] and [[D034 People search offers only what a card shows and trusts only quotes it can find]].

## AI who to meet ranking (F25, built on the AI foundation, in progress)
At an event that is on, once you have turned on Be easy to find and saved what you want ("I want to meet someone who..."), a "Rank with AI" button sits under "People you might want to meet". Pressing it re-orders the people already offered to you by meaning ("climate tech" finds a battery recycling lead). It never adds anyone: the people are the same ones the usual list can show you (same event, on for both, checked in, presence on, nobody blocked), and the model sees only each person's line, headline, tags and the line they wrote for the event, never a name or photo. People who switched AI search off are not sent to the model and stay in the list in the usual order after the AI's picks. The result is labelled "Improved with AI", with a "Usual list" button to go back. 3 an event on Free, 10 on Plus; the same question again within 10 minutes is free. If AI is off, used up or fails, the usual list stays with a plain message.

## Feedback summary for hosts (F23, built on the AI foundation, in progress)
At the top of an event's Feedback tab and in the Report: a short AI summary of the written answers, with the figures worked out by Lanyard, what worked, what to change (each with "about N people") and a few word-for-word quotes. Never names anyone, and nothing at all below 5 written answers to one question. Written once automatically when feedback closes (the owner gets the paragraph by email) and again on request, 3 a day. Included for every host.
