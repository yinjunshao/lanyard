# Screens and Routes

From `src/router/index.js`. All views are code-split so public pages stay light (target under 50 KB JS).

## Public
| Route | View |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Landing. Signed-in people (browser hint `lanyard-signed-in`) are sent to `/app`; `/?about=1` shows it anyway (Settings, "About Lanyard"). The `lanyard.` logo on other pages goes to `/app` when signed in |
| `/c/:cardId` | Public card (what a scan opens; Connect) |
| `/e/:code` | Event join / check-in |
| `/e/:code/feedback` | Feedback survey |
| `/hosts` | Host pitch + early pricing |
| `/terms`, `/privacy` | Legal |
| `/claim`, `/signin` | Account claim and sign-in |

**Search, link previews and tab titles (F37, [[D042 A shared card link shows what the card already shows, and is never indexed]]).** Only `/`, `/hosts`, `/terms` and `/privacy` may be indexed (they are in the sitemap). A link to `/c/:cardId` previews the person's name, headline and photo exactly as the card page shows them, `/e/:code` previews the event name and host; both send `noindex` and a made-up, deleted or ended one gets the generic Lanyard preview. Each page also sets its own browser-tab title ("Terms of service | Lanyard", "Ada Lovelace on Lanyard"; signed-in pages "People | Lanyard" and so on, set in `AppShell.vue`).

## Attendee app (`/app`, bottom tabs: My Card, Scan, People, Events)
`/app`, `/app/cards`, `/app/profile`, `/app/scan`, `/app/people`, `/app/people/:id`, `/app/events`, `/app/events/:id`, `/app/settings`, `/app/feedback/:eventId`

**Card page, In common** (F16, `/c/:cardId`): under the name and headline, one line with a small "In common" label: the first two facts joined by " · ", the third folds to "+1" under 600 px. Then Come to me for, About, Projects, Find online; a shared selfie and its prompts now follow the links. The People list rows do not show it.

**Draft a message** (F22, renamed in F47, a bottom sheet on `/c/:cardId` once you are connected, loaded only when the button is pressed): choices as chips in groups (Send it on, Tone, Goal, Talk about, You can offer), the note switch, "Anything to add?" with a counter, then Draft it. The result is a notice ("A draft written by AI..." or "Here's a starting point. ..."), an optional Subject, an editable Message, Copy and Open in, Try again and Change the choices, "2 drafts left today", and Mark as followed up. No new route.

**Feedback summary** (F23, host site, `/events/:id` Feedback tab and Report tab, in the same chunk as the rest of the page): a panel with the figures, the paragraph, "What worked", "What to change", "In their words", a footer "Written by AI from N written answers on D" with a link to all the answers, and Regenerate and Copy with "2 of 3 left today". States: below the threshold, ready to write, written, AI unavailable (a message; figures and answers unaffected), none left today.

**Do I know someone who** (F24, a bottom sheet on `/app/people`, no new route): a row under the search box opens a sheet with one text box (200 characters), example chips, Search, and "N searches left today". Results: a notice "Found by AI from people you know. It only uses what they've shared with you.", then up to 5 rows (photo, name, line, reason, "Matched on: ...", where and when you met) linking to the card; "Nobody fits yet. Try different words."; or, when AI is not used, "Showing a keyword search instead" with the reason. The sheet is a separate chunk (`PeopleAsk`, about 3.4 KB gzip) fetched when the row is pressed, so it is in no first load.

**Rank with AI** (F25, on the event hub at `/app/events/:id`, no new route): under "People you might want to meet", once a wish is saved. The button says how many are left for this event; pressing it is the only trigger. Result: the list re-ordered, "Improved with AI. Ranked from what people here show. Not right? Press Usual list."; or "AI didn't find anyone who fits better, so this is the usual list."; or, when AI is off, used up or failing, the usual list with the plain message. It lives in the existing lazy `EventPresence.vue` chunk; no chunk, route or shared file was added or changed.

**Who to meet** (F60, no new route): the check-in page `/e/:code` has a "Who to meet" panel (the intention field with rotating examples and the Suggest me tick, ticked, with the notice) unless the host switched suggestions off; the event hub has "Who to meet" under the figures while the event is on: a row of up to five people (tap for a sheet with "Find them in the room and scan their badge to connect." and Report or block), "Ranked with AI" when it was, Refresh, and below them your intention and the tick; Settings, Privacy and data lists "Suggestions at events" with a tick per event; Create event step 3 and Details have "Suggest people to each other" (on); the host's live tab shows "Wrote what they hope for" and "Not suggested" from 3 up.
**Tips** (F59, no new route): a dim layer with a ring round the real control and a small card (a sheet on a phone) runs on People (`data-tip="ai-ask"`), a connected person's page `/c/:id` (Follow up, private note, Draft a message), an event's page (Who to meet, intention, Suggest me), Your cards (New card, the first card's Edit) and My card (the home-screen panel, only where it shows). Settings > Help has "Show tips again". The admin page has a "Tips, last 30 days" table.

**Be easy to find** (F17; since F60 only the photo for Who to meet, its wish field and people row moved to Who to meet, in the event hub above the Room, Album and Perks tabs, only while the event is on and the room is on): a card that says who sees it, what they see and when it is deleted, with "Show me to people here" (off by default), a selfie, a line, "I want to meet someone who..." with topic chips, and a row of people (photo, first name and last initial, line, reason). Tapping one opens a sheet with a bigger photo and "Find them in the room and scan their badge to connect." plus Report or block. After the event ends the card only offers to delete your photo and line now.

**Card code** (F33): the code panel (large code, Plus "Choose your own code", the Plus line, "Replace with a random code" with its confirm, the next-change date) is a section of each card's editor in My cards (`/app/cards`), acting on that card. My card shows the note under the badge and a quiet line "Code ABC1 · Change in My cards"; the link is `/app/cards?edit=<cardId>&section=code`, which opens that card's editor at its code and survives a refresh and Back.

**Events tab** (F15, `/app/events`): three groups (F68): "In progress" at the top, "Upcoming" (soonest first, each with its "Starts 18:00") and "Past events" (newest attended first, rows with "Attended Wed 4 Nov 2026" and the count); every row has a status chip (In progress with a dot, Upcoming, Ended; one `EventStatusChip` component, no animation), and cards show venue, "Checked in 18:04" and the connection count. **Host dashboard "Your events" (F68, `host.<site>/`):** the same three groups ("In progress", "Upcoming", "Ended", empty ones left out), the same chips and the same status rule (the server clock comes back in `GET /api/host/me` as `now`, with each event's `endedAt`); the row action is "Live ›" in progress, "Open ›" upcoming, "Report ›" ended. A small dot with hidden text sits on the tab while an event you checked in to is ongoing. **Event hub** (`/app/events/:id`, where check-in lands): a back arrow (to My card, or to the Events list if you came from it), the event name, venue and status word, a figures row (Connections, Checked in, People here), the feedback prompt, People you met here, then the Room, Album and Perks tabs and a "Your choices at this event" row that opens a sheet. A removed event shows "Not available". See [[F15 Events tab and event hub]] and [[D025 Event status is worked out from the clock, never stored]].

**Welcome steps** (`/app/welcome?step=1` to `6`, F14): where a new card is made. Not an `/app` page: no tab bar, no account banner and no Terms pop-up over it. A progress bar, Back (Leave on step 1), Skip on steps 2 to 5, and one Continue in the thumb bar (on step 1, for someone who has not agreed yet, the F51 line sits directly above it: "By pressing Continue, you confirm you're 18 or over and agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy."). Each step saves as it is answered and the place is kept on the server, so closing the tab and opening `/app` carries on where they were; the step is also in the address, so a refresh and the browser Back button stay on the right step. Landing "Make your card", Sign in, the redeem page and My card with no card all open it, and `/app/profile` with no card redirects to it. People who left the steps are asked again from My card ("Pick up where you left off") and, for a guest who connected first, from People. "Finish your card" on My card opens the step that asks for the missing item (`?step=N`).

**Edit profile** (`/app/profile`, now only for editing a card): under the heading a quiet "Profile N of 6" bar with the missing items as links that scroll to and focus their fields (F49; "Your profile is complete" at 6); a Student / Career switch sits under Name. Student shows University, Course, Year; Career shows Company, Role and an optional "Studied at" (F11). The switch is on the profile, not in Settings. University and Studied at are a searchable picker with a "Not listed? Use ..." row and, for a `.ac.uk` email, a "Looks like you're at ... Use it" suggestion (F12).

**AI (F21):** Settings, Privacy and data has the AI search switch; Settings, Plan lists the AI allowances; `/admin` has an AI section (state, spend, kill switch, rollout stage, allow-list, test call).

**Settings** (F09) is a list of categories, each its own page: `/app/settings/account`, `/security` (Sign-in and devices: the Passkeys list with Rename, Remove and "Add a passkey on this device", F43; the authenticator app; devices), `/notifications` (Email and notifications), `/privacy` (Privacy and data), `/plan`, `/appearance` (Appearance and app), `/help` (Help and legal). Log out sits under the list. Phones show the list, then one page with a "‹ Settings" back link; from 900 px the list stays on the left. It is one route, `/app/settings/:page?`; `Settings.vue` loads the page. Old `/app/settings#plus`, `#signin` and `#install` links redirect to `/plan`, `/security` and `/appearance`. Emails about sign-in link to `/app/settings/security`.

## Host portal (its own site, F10)
Served at the root of `host.lanyd.fyi` (locally `http://host.localhost:5173`), from `src/router/routes.host.js`:
`/` (sign in by email code, then details, Host Terms and own name or organisation, then the dashboard), `/signin` (same page), `/events/:id` (live, details, feedback, report), `/events/:id/present` (F19: the projector page: event name, venue and time, a QR at least 40% of the screen height, the code in large figures, the typed address and a check-in count; Fullscreen, Light/Dark and Exit show on movement and hide after 3 s; refreshes every 15 s, not while hidden), `/events/:id/print` (F19: the A4 poster, black on white with a thin strap rule; opens the print dialog on load, `?auto=0` stops that), `/billing`, `/redeem` and `/redeem/:code` (F20: a host promo code for event credits, in the Billing chunk), `/account` (sign-in email, devices, organisation, team, integrations), `/signout` (signs out and reloads to `/`). Old `/host/...` addresses on the host site redirect to the root; anything else goes to the main site.

On the main site, `/host` and `/host/...` go to the same page on the host site (the router, and a 301 from the Worker). `/hosts` (the pitch page) stays on the main site and links to the portal. `/admin` stays on the main site.

**Create event in steps (F30):** on the host dashboard, New event opens `/?step=1`; the five steps are Basics, Look, At the event, Questions and Review (`?step=2` to `?step=5`). "Step N of 5" and the step names show where you are, Back and Next sit in a bar at the bottom, Cancel is at the top; the browser Back button moves between steps and a refresh stays on the step. The Details tab of a saved event stays one page.

**Company branding (F66, [[D061 Host branding shows on paid events only, keeps a Check in with Lanyard line, and takes PNG, JPEG or WebP logos up to 1 MB]]):** no new route. Host site, Account and team: a Branding section (display name, logo Add, Replace and Remove, a preview of the Present head and the check-in head), changeable by the owner only. Create event step 2 and the Details tab, under Advanced: "Use our company branding", on for a paid event of an organisation with branding, shown disabled with the reason otherwise ("Branding shows on paid events" on a free one). On a paid event with it on: the check-in page (`/e/:code`) has the name on the strap, the logo above the event name and "Check in with Lanyard" at the foot; the event hub (`/app/events/:id`) has the logo or name on its head and the line at the foot; Present has the logo or the name in place of `lanyard.` and the line at the foot; the poster has the logo or name at the top and the line in place of the wordmark. The admin page (`/admin`) has a Company branding section (show logo, remove, switch off and on).

**Event themes and an advanced look (F65):** no new route. Step 2 of Create event and the Details tab show eight themes (Persimmon, Formal, Night, Forest, Ocean, Plum, Rose, Sand) as a radio group of small check-in heads, two across on a phone and four from 560 px, one tab stop, arrow keys, the chosen one ringed; the old select and native colour input are gone. **Advanced** (a button that opens a `UiReveal`, shut unless a brand colour or text colour is already set) has the brand colour (the F55 swatches and colour panel, free for hosts: the Plus gate is a prop that is off here and on for cards), the text on the strap (Automatic, White, Dark; one that would be hard to read is not offered, and one that stops reading when the colour or theme changes goes back to Automatic with a line saying so) and a disabled "Use our company branding" switch that says it is coming soon ([[F66 Host branding on event pages]]). The theme is drawn on the check-in head, the Present page (stripe, a tinted page in the light look, dark to begin with for Night) and the printed poster (its top rule), by CSS only; the check-in page gets no script for it. Review lists a chosen text colour. A copy of an earlier event keeps its own look and shows no grid.

**New events start from the latest settings (F64):** no new route. A fresh Create event form starts with "Show the live attendee room", "Suggest people to each other", the feedback tick and "What this event collects" (the list, the email and card ticks, the why line) as on the organisation's most recently created event; a first event starts from the fixed defaults (the three on, collects off). Each setting that differs from the fixed default has a muted line under it, "As on your last event"; the collects switch, when it comes on that way, says "On because your last event collected names. Untick to stop." in bold; Review adds "(as on your last event)" to those rows. With "Start from an earlier event" they come from that event and the lines say "the event you started from". A draft keeps its own values. See [[D056 New events start from the organisation's latest event's switches, collects included, flagged on the form]].

**Create event form refresh (F63):** no new route. A step slides in from the side you are going towards (Back slides the other way; a short fade under reduced motion), and each step is labelled groups with one help line a field and a five-part progress row. Step 3 has **Perks** as rows (a narrow Perk box and a wide Details (optional) box, Remove on each row, Add a perk, ten at most; the two boxes stack under about 480 px of form width) and **Food and drink** as a tick, "Food or drinks provided", that eases open to a text line and six option ticks (Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Kosher, Gluten-free, Dairy-free). The same two are on the Details tab. There is no Access field on Create, Details, Review, the check-in page or the event hub (the value is still stored and returned by the API). The check-in page and the hub print one "Food and drink" row, such as "Pizza. Options: Vegan, Halal". The hub also shows the dress code and good-to-know lines when the host wrote them.

**Check-in page layout (F67):** no new route or words. `/e/:code` and the host's preview draw the head, the notice (now straight under the head), the info list, then up to three panels in this order, each left out when it has nothing: **About you** (the name for a guest and the host's questions), **At this event** (the attendee room tick, then Who to meet with the intention and Suggest me) and **Sharing** (Sponsors, then This event collects details), then the muted totals line, any error and the Check in bar with the F51 line. One gap between panels, one between rows, every tick a full-width row of at least 48 px. What starts ticked is as before: Suggest me and the collects tick on, sponsors and the attendee room off. See [[F67 Check-in page layout]].

**Named attendance (F31):** no new route. Step 3 of Create event and an event's Details tab have a "What this event collects" section (a tick for the list, two optional extras, a required why line); the Live tab shows a "Who checked in" list with a CSV link for an event that has it on (host event chunk), and the Report tab links to the CSV. On the main site the check-in page `/e/:code` shows "This event collects" above Check in for such an event; the event hub's "Your choices at this event" sheet and Settings, Privacy and data show the lists a person is on, with the same ticks, saved at once.
**Drops (F32):** no new route. The event hub (`/app/events/:id`) has a **For you** section (only when the organiser made a drop) with Show, Copy and Hide for a key or code and "Show voucher", which opens a sheet with the event, the title, your first name and photo and a live clock (staff mark it used there with a PIN). The host event page has a **Drops** tab (make, counts, stop, add more, download the unused, delete, who has it). Both are drawn inside the existing event chunks.


**Previews (F18):** on the host portal, the Create event form and an event's Details tab show the check-in page as attendees will see it, beside the form from 1000 px and behind a Preview button (full screen) below that, with a Default / Formal / Night strip and a brand colour that change the form too. In Edit profile and My cards, a Preview shows your badge or "What they see" (the card page) while you type: a Preview button at the top below 1260 px, open beside the page from 1260 px. Nothing in a preview is saved or sent. A host's Formal or Night theme, or brand colour, shows as a strap on the check-in page head.

## Development only## Development only
`/ui-test` shows every picker for checking by eye. It is registered only under `vite dev`; a production build does not contain it.

## Two route tables
`src/router/index.js` picks `routes.attendee.js` or `routes.host.js` by `ON_HOST_SITE`, so each site's router names only its own pages. Host API calls live in `src/lib/hostapi.js`, which only host pages import. See [[D020 Host accounts separate from attendee accounts]].
