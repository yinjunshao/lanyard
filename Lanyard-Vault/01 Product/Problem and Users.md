# Problem and Users

## The problem
- People meet dozens of others at events and remember almost none.
- Swapping socials means typing usernames and finding the right account.
- Nobody remembers what a contact is good at, so connections go unused.
- Shy attendees do not know who to approach.
- Hosts cannot show what impact an event had; sign-in is paper or Eventbrite lists.

## Users
| User | Need | Pays? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student / attendee | Remember people; share only chosen links | No (optional Plus, see [[Pricing]]) |
| Society committee | Check-in, proof of attendance for sponsors | Free tier, then small packs |
| Careers service / students' union | Engagement data for employability reporting | Yes, termly plan (target) |
| Professional organiser | Lighter, cheaper alternative to event apps | Yes, per event |
| Sponsor / recruiter | Opt-in access to interested students | Yes (later, likely largest revenue) |
