# Metrics and Kill Criteria

## Metrics that matter
- Share of attendees who check in at a hosted event
- Average connections per attendee (vs a paper-card / WeChat baseline)
- Share of connections with a selfie
- Return visits within 7 and 30 days
- Hosts who rebook; hosts who pay

## Decision thresholds (from the docs)
- **Proceed** if at one real event at least 40% of pairs who scan both save the connection and at least 1 in 3 open a follow-up within 48h. (Opportunity brief)
- **Rethink** if fewer than 2 of 5 pilot organisers say they would pay £100+ for the report. (Strategy doc; prices there were higher than current list prices, so re-set this to a number matching [[Pricing]]. See [[Open Questions]].)
- **Rethink** if people scan once, never return, and see it as "just a QR card".

## Data to log per pilot
Event name, host, date, attendees, check-ins, connections, selfie rate, 7-day return, host quote, willingness to pay.
