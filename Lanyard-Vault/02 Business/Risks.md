# Risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retention outside events | Biggest risk; Lanyard may be used once | Event album, "You met N people" prompt, People search, feedback follow-ups |
| Sales is a real job | Need organisers, demos, live support; high stakes if it breaks | Run setup yourself; start with 3-5 pilots; consider a business co-founder |
| Crowded market | Swapcard, Brella, Popl, HiHello, LinkedIn | Win on price, no download, selfie memory, student fit |
| Privacy / GDPR | Selfies are the most sensitive data; sponsor leads cause complaints | Dual consent, delete either side, aggregate-only, per-sponsor opt-in. See [[Privacy and Data Retention]] |
| Under-18 users | Safety, Discovery mode | 18+ rule, no Discovery for under-18. See [[D012 18 plus only]] |
| Solo founder bandwidth | Support during live events | Narrow scope, free tier caps |
| Trademark | No search done on "Lanyard" | Do one before commercial reliance. See [[Brand and Logo]] |
| Legal readiness | ICO fee, DPA, privacy policy | Register with ICO, draft DPA, lawyer/legal clinic review before first paid event |
| Revenue projections are invented | Could mislead decisions | Use pilot data only. See [[Metrics and Kill Criteria]] |
| Platform dependency | Everything on Cloudflare | Standard stack; KV/D1/R2 export scripts exist |
