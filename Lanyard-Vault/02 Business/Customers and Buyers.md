# Customers and Buyers

| Segment | Budget reality | How to win | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student societies | Small; free tier carries them | Free first events; sponsor pays add-on | Committee member with university email + proof society exists (union listing or staff email); admin approves |
| Universities / institutions | Real budgets, slow cycles | Free pilot, then report data; termly plan | Approval + university email |
| Careers services / unions | Employability reporting needs | Cohort reports for funders | Quote |
| Companies / organisers | Pay standard price | Cheaper, lighter than Swapcard, Brella, Grip; no download | Pay up front |
| Sponsors / recruiters | Pay for access to students | Booth QR + opt-in leads | Per-sponsor consent |

## Buyer objections to prepare for
- "WeChat / LinkedIn / paper cards are enough." Answer with measured pilot data (exchanges per attendee, 7-day follow-through).
- "What happens to our attendees' data?" Aggregate only; DPA; see [[Privacy and Data Retention]].
- "What if it breaks at our event?" Run the setup yourself; test on the day. See [[Risks]].
