# Competitors

Evidence checked 2026-10-14 (company sites) per the opportunity brief. Re-verify before quoting.

## Digital business card apps
| Product | Position now | Gap Lanyard fills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Popl | "AI GTM platform for event lead capture"; B2B, CRM sync, hardware | One-way lead capture; not peer-to-peer; not student |
| HiHello | "Professional presence platform" for enterprise brands | No "how we met" memory; not student |
| Blinq, Linq, Tappy, vCard-QR, Beaconstac | Generic cards, NFC/QR, some hardware | Mostly one-way share |

## Event networking / lead tools
Swapcard, Brella, Bizzabo, Grip, PheedLoop, Whova, Cvent: matchmaking, analytics, CRM export, enterprise prices. Lanyard's angle: no-download, tiny price, 50-300 person events they ignore.

## Student networking
Handshake (careers/jobs, institutional), LinkedIn (premature/careerist for freshers; QR exists), PeopleGrove/Gravyty (licensed alumni mentoring), Unibuddy (recruitment). None owns the in-the-moment freshers/fair ritual.

## The wedge
Contextual memory (selfie + note + "Come to me for"), ritual fit (badge/lanyard moment), zero install. The durable moat is the **contextual connection graph**, not the QR renderer (commodity).

## Positioning lines
- "Popl and HiHello capture a one-way sales lead; Lanyard saves a two-way relationship with the context of how you met."
- "LinkedIn is for people you already know. Lanyard is for the person you met tonight."
