# Glossary

- **Card:** a shareable badge with its own QR, note, links, tags and theme.
- **Come to me for:** tags saying what a person can help with.
- **Strap:** the coloured band on the badge; the one accent colour.
- **Host:** an organisation or person running events. **Org:** a host organisation (society, institution, company, individual).
- **Credit:** one event of up to 300 check-ins.
- **Check-in:** scanning the event QR to join the room.
- **Room:** live directory of opted-in attendees at an event.
- **Discovery mode:** matching with others who want to meet people (planned; not for under-18s).
- **Pilot:** a free event run to gather evidence.
- **Activity Mode:** earlier name for the host/organiser layer.
- **Claim:** a guest turning their session into a full account with a passkey.
- **RP_ID:** WebAuthn relying-party domain; fixes where a passkey works.
- **B2B2C:** businesses pay, consumers use for free.
