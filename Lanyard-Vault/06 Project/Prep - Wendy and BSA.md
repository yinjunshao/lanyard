# Prep: BSA interview
Bloomsbury Startup Academy · UCL Entrepreneurs Society · reading week

---

## What BSA is
- In-house pre-accelerator run by UCL Entrepreneurs Society
- Turns ideas into workable business models and plans
- Intensive, building and iterating from week one (8 weeks)
- Interview is informal; book the earliest slot; teammates must attend

---

## Lanyard in one line
A scan-to-connect digital card for the people you meet at events, so the connections don't disappear after the event.

- Live as a mobile web app at lanyd.fyi
- Professor supporting with resources
- Student ambassadors: UCL, Imperial, Warwick, Bristol; QMUL and US talks in progress

---

## 1. The problem and validation
- People, especially students forgetting each other the second they part in a networking event, forgetting their name, face and what they do. This makes any social media connection worthless as no real connection is made. We cannot fix people's memory, therefore we made a tool that aids people 
- Validation: [number of students spoken to] · [scans / sign-ups] · [what they said]

## 2. Why me, and my edge
- I have shipped: Lanyard live, plus a portfolio of software products
- Design training from Xiaomi Automotive and Sevnce Robotics internships
- I'm the user: a UCL student at these events every week
- Ambassador network across universities

---

## 3. Who it affects and first target
- First target: university students at society and careers events
- Assumptions to test: they want something lighter than LinkedIn; they scan at events; they return afterwards

## 4. Two to three years out
- Default way people connect in person at universities, then conferences
- Revenue: event organisers and societies, plus premium individual features

---

## 5. Challenges and pivots
- Cold start: no value until the other person scans
- Retention: people may not return after the event
- Competition: LinkedIn QR, Popl, Blinq. Our angle is students, events, follow-up
- Pivot: sell to societies (B2B), or focus on post-event follow-up

## 6. Why BSA and what help I need
- Turn ambassador interest into measurable usage
- Choose the retention metric that matters
- Reach societies and event organisers
- Pressure-test the business model

## 7. Eight weeks, all in
- Weekly cadence: [N] events per week, measure scans, connections and return visits
- Ship one improvement each week
- Already balance a degree with several projects

---

## Interview tips
- Have a live demo on your phone and offer to scan them
- Lead with numbers, even small ones
- About a minute per answer; let them dig in
- Mention the teammates who will attend
