# D006 UK campus beachhead first

**Status:** Accepted (in practice)  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-16

## Context
Strategy feedback was written with China-style pricing (CNY) and WeChat in mind. Founder is at UCL with free access to organisers.

## Decision
Start with UK university events, priced in pounds. China later via a localised partner. Confirm explicitly if this changes.

## Consequences
Pricing, legal (UK GDPR, ICO) and sales channels all UK-first.
