# D013 No hardware or NFC for now

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-14

## Context
Popl and Tappy already compete on hardware; fulfilment adds cost a solo student cannot support.

## Decision
Stay web and QR only. NFC tap is a later idea.

## Consequences
Lower cost and risk; less 'physical' feel.
