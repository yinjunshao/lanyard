# D001 Hosts pay, attendees free

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-16

## Context
Individuals almost never pay for a networking app. Event organisers have budgets and are measured on networking; sponsors buy on that data. Organiser adoption also solves cold start.

## Decision
Keep the attendee product free. Charge hosts (per event, packs, termly plans) and later sponsors. Connecting, selfie and a basic profile are never paywalled.

## Consequences
Revenue depends on selling to organisers, a real sales job. Attendee product is a distribution surface. A Plus personal tier exists but is low priority.
