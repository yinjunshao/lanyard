# D054 Everyone checked in can be suggested unless they untick Suggest me, shown only what their card shows

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-11-24  ·  Narrows [[D027 Event presence is opt-in, private and deleted 48 hours after the event]]

## Context
The owner asked (2026-11-24) for an optional intention at check-in ("What are you hoping to get from this event?") and AI recommendations of who to meet "depending on everyone's intentions and everyone's profile". Asked who can appear in other people's suggestions, the owner chose **everyone checked in**, over "only people who wrote an intention" and "keep the host switch" ([[F60 Intention at check-in and who to meet for everyone]]). Under [[D027 Event presence is opt-in, private and deleted 48 hours after the event]] being findable at an event was opt-in and off by default. This shows people to strangers in a room without their having chosen it, so under UK GDPR it can rest on legitimate interests only if people are told at check-in, what is shown is limited to what their card already shows, and they can say no at once and later.

## Decision
- **D027 is narrowed, not dropped.** Everyone checked in to an event can be suggested to the others there, while the event is on, unless they untick "Suggest me to people here". Be easy to find (F17) stays opt-in and off by default; since F60 it only adds a face photo to the person's entry. The photo is shown only to people who may see that person in Who to meet at that moment (`canViewSuggestPhoto`), and only where the room is on (F17's own gate).
- **Told at check-in, on by default, untickable.** The check-in page shows the optional intention field and the tick, ticked to start with: "Suggest me to people here. Others checked in see your name, headline and tags, and your intention if you write one." with small print (first name and last initial only, no card, links or contact details; a photo only with Be easy to find; you can still check in; change it any time; deleted 48 hours after the event). The words and their version live in `src/lib/suggest.js`, read by the page and the Worker.
- **Nothing is assumed.** A row in `event_suggest` exists only for someone who was shown the notice: a check-in that carries a real true or false, or a choice on the hub or in Settings. Someone who checked in before F60, or through an old page or a script, is in nobody's list until they choose on the hub (where the tick then starts unticked, because nothing was shown to them before).
- **Recorded.** Each choice is a `consent_log` row (scope `event_suggest:<event>`, `granted`, `refused` or `withdrawn`, `ref` `checkin`, `hub` or `settings`, the exact words and the version). The current state is the row, deleted 48 hours after the event ends; the history rows stay 6 years like every record of a choice.
- **Only what the card shows.** An entry is exactly `{ id, name, headline, tags, intention, reason }` and `photo` only with Be easy to find: first name and last initial, the default card's headline, at most three tags it shows, their own intention, one reason. Read through `visibleFields()`, so a field a profile keeps but hides never shows or matches. `id` is the row's opaque id, never a user id; no card link, link, email or contact route.
- **Who sees whom.** Only people checked in to the same event, while it is on for both (the shared status rule), with the host's switch on, both with Suggest me ticked (someone who unticks sees no list either, as in F17: nobody looks without being seen), not blocked in either direction. Another event's people never.
- **The host's switch.** `events.suggest_on`, "Suggest people to each other", on by default, in Create event step 3 and Details; a copy keeps the earlier event's setting. Off: no section, the list route refuses with no people. It replaces the room (`directory_on`) as the gate for who to meet; the room keeps its own meaning.
- **AI order.** When AI is on for the viewer, the model re-orders the same people from the viewer's intention and card and each candidate's intention and visible fields, each candidate's text inside the `<data>` fence, never a name, photo or any id; a field that reads like an instruction is left out; a pick stands only with a quote really in that person's record (F24's `verifyMatches`). It runs **by itself when the list is opened**, at most once per person per event every 20 minutes (one conditional write on the viewer's own row), inside F25's allowance (`who_to_meet`, 3 per event Free, 10 Plus) and the foundation's limits. The checked picks are kept 20 minutes in `ai_cache` and checked again against the people gathered on every request. Without AI, or when it fails, is over budget or not allowed, the plain order is used and no "Ranked with AI" label shows. This is a stated exception to layer 1 of [[AI Safety and Cost Plan]] ("no AI call on page load"), bounded by the 20 minutes and the per-event quota.
- **Hosts** see two counts on the live view, how many wrote an intention and how many unticked, each hidden under 3 ([[D004 Aggregate-only host analytics]]). No host-facing file reads the table per person.
- **Retention.** Rows go 48 hours after the event ends (the daily purge, the F17 end rule), with the account (cascade), and in the host reset. In the person's data download as `eventSuggestions` (their rows and every choice with its words and version).

> [!warning] Owner decision 2026-11-24: everyone checked in to an event can be suggested to the others there, whether or not they wrote an intention. This narrows [[D027 Event presence is opt-in, private and deleted 48 hours after the event]], where being findable was opt-in and off by default. The risk: people are shown to strangers in the room without having chosen it. Under UK GDPR this can rest on legitimate interests only if people are told at check-in, what is shown is limited to what their card already shows, and they can say no at once and later. So this item builds: a notice on the check-in page, a "Suggest me to people here" tick that is on by default and can be unticked at check-in and any time after, nothing shown that the person's card does not already show, no photo unless they turned on Be easy to find, blocks both ways, and a host switch to turn suggestions off for an event. A legitimate interests note is added to the audit. The Privacy Policy needs a new section, which the owner must approve with the version bump. A lawyer's look before launch is advised.

## Consequences
A person who checks in without reading the page is suggested to the room. What they show is what anyone they hand their card to already sees, minus their surname, links and contact details; the risk is being approached by someone unwanted, which blocking (both ways, from the list) and the untick answer. The Privacy Policy has a new section 8f; the Terms and Privacy version was not bumped (owner's decision, flagged as material). F17's own list (`GET /api/e/:id/suggestions`) and F25's button route stay in the Worker but no page calls them; a later clean-up can remove them. The legitimate interests assessment is in [[UK GDPR Audit 2026-11-01]].
