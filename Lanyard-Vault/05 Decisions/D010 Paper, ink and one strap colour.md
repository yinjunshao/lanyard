# D010 Paper, ink and one strap colour

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Context
Avoid generic AI-app look; the card should feel like a real badge and work at banking events, hackathons and socials.

## Decision
Paper #F4F1EA, ink #1B1A17, one accent Persimmon #C4441E (the strap; was #D9502B until 2026-11-24, deepened in F55.0 so white reads at 5.0:1), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, no UI library, themes change only the strap/paper-ink.

## Consequences
Distinct identity; the earlier volt/ink deck look is stale.
