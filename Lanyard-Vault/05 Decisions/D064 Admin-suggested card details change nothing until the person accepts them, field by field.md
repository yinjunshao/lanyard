# D064 Admin-suggested card details change nothing until the person accepts them, field by field

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-11-26  ·  Owner decision 2026-11-25 ([[F75 Admin-suggested profile edits that the person approves]]); builds on [[D063 Admin-made profiles are private until claimed, by a hashed link and an emailed code, and deleted after 30 days]]

## Context
The owner wants to fill in details for someone whose card is empty, from what they made public elsewhere (for example LinkedIn), and tell them by email. Their card is public, so writing data from an outside source onto it is a new use of that data that they never chose. Asked how admin edits should work, the owner chose **"They approve first"**: the details are a suggestion, and nothing changes on the card until the person presses Accept. Email only to verified addresses.

## Decision
- **Kept apart.** A suggestion is a row in `profile_suggestions` (migration 0047): `user_id` (one per person, cascades with the account), `id` (new for each suggestion), `fields` (JSON of only the suggested fields, in Edit profile's shape), `photo_key`, `source`, `created_at`, `expires_at` (30 days), `emailed_at`. Nothing reads it except the person's own routes, their own data export and the admin routes. It is not in the card, the card's code, the link preview, people search, People, Who to meet, presence, the event room, host views, anyone else's export or the recovery search (tested by searching each answer for the suggested values).
- **An allow-list.** The request may carry only `name`, `mode`, `uni`, `institutionId`, `course`, `year`, `company`, `role`, `headline`, `about`, `tags`, `links`, `photoDataUrl` and `source`. Any other key (email, plan, settings, ids, `showAge`, projects, a photo key, anything unknown) is `400 not_allowed` and nothing is stored. Values are cleaned by Edit profile's own `profilePayload` and the same mode and institution checks when they are suggested, and fields that would not change the card are left out.
- **The person accepts through the Edit profile save.** `PUT /api/profile` now reads its body with `readProfileEdit` and writes with `profileEditStmt` (`worker/profileFields.js`). Accept selected builds the same body from the ticked fields and calls the same two functions (the same checks, the same cleaning, the same photo upload and its 20-an-hour limit), so the same messy body saved through Edit profile and accepted through a suggestion stores the same profile. Sensitive words (D057) are treated exactly as in an Edit profile save: stored as typed, screened later by matching; no new screen here.
- **One conditional write.** Accept is one batch gated on the suggestion's `id` still being the open one: the outcome count, the profile update, the photo queued for deletion and the row deleted. Decline is the same without the profile update. The page sends the `id` it showed; an old one is 409. Two accepts at once, accept and decline at once, or an accept and a new suggestion at once give one winner and a consistent profile (tested on a database where every query waits a turn).
- **One open suggestion per person.** A new one replaces the old one in one write that also queues the old photo for deletion (F66's `media_deletes`).
- **The photo.** Stored under `suggest/`, which `/media` refuses (400), so it is never public; the person sees it through `GET /api/profile/suggested/photo` and the admin through the admin route, both `no-store`. On Accept it is uploaded again as an ordinary avatar through Edit profile's photo path; the `suggest/` copy is deleted on Accept, Decline, Withdraw, replacement, the purge and erasure.
- **The email.** Only to `email_verified = 1` with a sign-in address, never to an unverified or pending one. It names the fields and the source, says nothing changes until they accept, links to `/app/profile/suggested`, and holds no value. Once per suggestion (`emailed_at` is set before sending, only when empty) and at most one a day per person, so a quick correction by the admin does not send a second one. It is a service message about their own account: the news-email opt-in does not apply, and there is no account-wide "no emails" switch to respect (checked). People with no verified email see only the banner on My card; that is the notice.
- **30 days and erasure.** The daily purge deletes suggestions past `expires_at` with their photos. `eraseUser` deletes the photo; the row cascades.
- **Counts only after a decision.** `suggestion_counts (day, outcome, n)` keeps how many were accepted, declined or expired per UTC day, for 400 days; the admin page shows the last 30 days and the metrics take `suggestions_open`, `suggestions_accepted_30d` and `suggestions_declined_30d` (`METRICS_VERSION` 3). The values and who decided are not kept, so the admin list shows open suggestions as waiting and the outcomes only as totals.
- **The F74 hand-off.** Claiming a made profile with an email that already has an account (after the code proves the address, so the page still never says which emails have accounts) turns the made profile's details and photo into a suggestion on that account (the one email goes if its address is verified), erases the made profile and its link, and signs nobody in. If that account has no profile, nothing is handed over and the F74 behaviour stays (the link keeps working).
- **Admin side.** Six routes under `/api/admin/suggest`, registered after the admin token check (401 without it, audited, try limit). `source` is required, one cleaned line of at most 80 characters (F74's `readSource`). The low-score filter scores cards with `profileScore` in the Worker, 500 at a time, as the metrics do: 2 of 6 or under. Admin-made profiles waiting to be claimed are left out (they are edited as F74's).

## Consequences
The owner can fill in an empty card in a few minutes, and the person decides, field by field, what goes on it; until then nothing about the suggestion is visible to anyone but them and the admin. A person with no verified email learns of it only when they next open My card, and a suggestion they never see is gone after 30 days. Decisions made without the owner: the one-a-day email cap; outcomes kept as counts, not as a per-person history; an accepted photo uploaded again as an avatar; suggestions only for accounts that have a profile; the hand-off does nothing for an existing account without a profile.
