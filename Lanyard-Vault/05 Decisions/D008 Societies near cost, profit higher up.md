# D008 Societies near cost, profit higher up

**Status:** Accepted  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Context
Societies have little money but carry Lanyard to many students; their events cost almost nothing to serve.

## Decision
Societies free for 1 event a week up to 200 check-ins, then £5 per event, £20 for 5, £25 term. Money comes from sponsors, institutions, companies and careers services. Approval and university email required.

## Consequences
Growth engine with capped loss. Needs verification to prevent abuse.
