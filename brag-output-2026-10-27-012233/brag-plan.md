# Lanyard for hosts — brag plan

**What it is:** Lanyard replaces sign-in sheets with a QR check-in. Attendees connect with a shared selfie; the host gets a live room and a report afterwards.
**For:** Student societies, careers services and unions, event organisers and companies.
**Sets it apart:** One QR at the door, no app, no typing. Live check-ins, connections and selfies. A post-event report (courses, come-to-me-for topics, check-in answers, sponsor opt-ins) that's aggregate only, so hosts never see who connected with whom.
**Most persuasive claim:** "Know who actually came — and who they met." Plus the pricing: attendees always free, first 3 society events on us.
**Visual hook:** A paper sign-in sheet filling with illegible scrawl.
**Real UI shown:** The host Live poster (check-in QR, code, Checked in / Connections / Selfies), the Report view with its chips, the host page's three steps and its pricing copy.
**Tone:** `polished` with `default` energy: confident and practical. Same paper/ink/Persimmon look as the attendee video, so the two read as a pair.
**Share caption:** "Ditch the sign-in sheet. One QR at the door, a live room, and a report you can show sponsors."

## Fictional stand-ins
Event "Spring Careers Fair" at "Main Hall", code K7QF. Sponsors "Northbeam" and "Halden Labs" are invented. Every number in the live room and report (148 check-ins, 212 connections, 96 selfies, course/topic counts) is illustrative UI data, not a claim about real usage.
